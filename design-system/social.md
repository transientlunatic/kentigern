# Social

Cards for sharing charts are a small product of their own, in the manner of the FT's salmon ground and the Economist's red tab: one recognisable frame, so a chart is attributable before anyone reads the credit.

## The frame

- **Ground.** `surface` with a 24px `grid-fine` graph-paper grid (light, Parchment) or the same on `dark`. It is the equivalent of a signature colour: pale, cool lines on a warm ground, after OS maps.
- **Tab.** The ripple mark in `primary`, 72px wide, hanging from the top edge 48px in from the left. It is the equivalent of the Economist's red bar and appears on every card in the same place.
- **Burned-in text.** Headline (serif italic, the finding), subtitle (what is plotted and per what), mono source line bottom-left and `daniel-williams.co.uk` bottom-right.
- **Chart.** Charts follow the Charts guide, with no gridlines of their own and labels of at least 18px at 1200px wide.

## Formats

1200 × 675 for links and timelines, 1080 × 1350 for portrait feeds, 1080 × 1080 for square. Keep a 48px margin. Export PNG, and always write alt text with the finding in words.
