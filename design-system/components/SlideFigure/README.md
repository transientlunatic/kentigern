Content slide for one published figure with up to three notes and its citation.

Title in `slide-h1`, top-left, one line, no full stop. Notes use `slide-body` in a left column of about 40% width; bold at most one key phrase with `slide-emphasis`. The figure sits on a `panel` tile with `radius-lg` and `space-4` inner padding, right column about 55%, at its native aspect ratio. The citation is the paper's arXiv address in `slide-ref`, bottom-right.

The consumer provides the figure image (a light-background PNG or SVG), the notes and the arXiv id. Dark-ground figures made in d3 skip the tile and sit directly on `surface`.
