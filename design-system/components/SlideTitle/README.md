Opening slide: title, one-line subtitle, presenter block, and ripple arcs in the bottom-right corner.

The consumer provides the title (`slide-title`, sentence case, one or two lines), a subtitle (`slide-body`, `ink-muted`, at most about 8 words) and the presenter lines. Arcs are 2px circles centred on the bottom-right corner at 64px steps, coloured `primary`, `indigo`, `gold`, `green`, then `rule`; they bleed off the slide. Leave an optional logo slot top-left, as tall as the title's cap height; omit it if you have none.

Do not add a background image or a second title. Do not centre the title.
