Full-bleed big-number slide: one figure and one label, centred.

Set the number in `slide-stat` and the label in `slide-stat-label`. The ground is `gold` by default, `red` for the one result the talk is about, or `blue`; text is the matching `on-gold`, `on-red` or `on-blue`. The consumer provides a number of at most four digits and a label of at most about 10 words that says what is being counted and over which runs.

Do not put a second number, a chart or an icon on a stat slide. Do not use more than three stat slides in a row. Slide number sits bottom-right in `slide-ref`.
