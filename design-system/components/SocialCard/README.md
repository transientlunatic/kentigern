Shareable chart card for social media: the chart with its headline, subtitle and source burned in, so it still makes sense when separated from the post.

Its recognisable parts are the **ripple tab** hanging from the top-left edge (the ripple mark in `primary`), the **graph-paper ground** (`surface` with a 24px `grid-fine` grid, so the grid is the ground and the chart draws no gridlines), and the **mono source line** with `daniel-williams.co.uk` bottom-right. Keep all four on every card; they are what makes a card recognisable at thumbnail size.

Size: 1200 × 675 (links and timelines), 1080 × 1350 (portrait) or 1080 × 1080 (square); outer margin 48px. Headline in `slide-title`'s serif italic at 52px, at most two lines, stated as the finding ("O4b added 161 binary black hole candidates"), not the topic. Subtitle in Lato 300, 26px, `ink-muted`. Chart labels at least 18px so they survive a phone screen; value labels 22px bold. Source line in mono 18px.

Use `light` (Parchment) by default and `dark` when a dark card suits the feed. The consumer provides the headline, subtitle, chart data and source. Do not add logos, borders, drop shadows or a second chart.
