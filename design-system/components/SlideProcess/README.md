Slide for a linear process of two to four steps.

Each step is a chevron in `primary` with `on-primary` text: a bold name (`slide-emphasis`) over one short line (`slide-body` at most 12 words). The step the talk dwells on is `gold` with `on-gold` text. Chevrons touch with an 8px overlap; the first has a flat left edge.

The consumer provides the steps in order. Do not add connecting arrows, numbers or icons; the chevron shape carries the direction.
